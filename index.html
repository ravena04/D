somewhere between Room 103 & forever ♡

To my favourite person in Room 103, ❤️

I don't really know how to write this without getting emotional. And honestly, I don't even know where to start.

How am I supposed to fit four years of us into one little letter? Four years of conversations, chaos, laughter, crying, gossip, roasts, movies, getting ready, random plans and all those tiny moments that somehow became my favourite memories.

Because you weren't just my roommate. You slowly became the person I automatically wanted to tell everything to.

"Dhiviii... inaiku enna nadathuchu theriyumaaa?"

And somehow, those became part of our everyday life. The kind of conversations that started with one tiny thing and somehow lasted forever.

You have genuinely been my all-time chatting partner, gossip partner, roasting department and emergency advice centre.

And your advice? Annoyingly, most of the time you were right. 😂

There were so many times when I came to you with something confusing, something stupid, something exciting, or something I just needed to talk about. And you were there.

Sometimes you gave advice. Sometimes you just listened. Sometimes you roasted me. Sometimes you made me laugh when I didn't even feel like laughing.

But you were there. And that mattered more than you probably realise.

Not just the big memories.
But you being there. ♡

When I think about these four years, I don't just remember one particular day.

I remember our first day. Our first conversations. Our Room 103 outings. Late-night gossip. Movie nights. Getting ready together. Saree disasters. 😂 Random dances. Karaoke nights. Exam panic. Entire days spent in a dark room. Eating together. Talking about love stories. Roasting each other. Laughing until we couldn't breathe. Crying when things got difficult.

And all those completely ordinary days that didn't feel special at the time.

I think that's what makes this so hard.

You don't realise you're living a memory while you're living it.

You only realise later that the random Tuesday, the late-night conversation, the silly gossip, the movie you almost didn't watch, the dinner we shared, the stupid joke we kept repeating... was actually one of the best parts of your life.

And somehow, you made these four years feel like home.

You came into my life as a roommate.
And somewhere along the way, you became family. ❤️

I know things are going to change now.

The room will be different. The routine will be different. The random conversations won't happen in quite the same way anymore.

There won't be someone right there for me to turn to and say,

"Wait... listen to this." 😂

And honestly? I'm going to miss that so badly.

I'm going to miss you more than this letter can say. Probably more than I even know how to say right now.

But there is one thing I don't want to change.

Even if life takes us in different directions, even if our days become busy, even if we don't get to talk every single day like we do now...

I still want to know what happened in your day. I still want the random updates. I still want the silly stories. I still want the gossip. I still want the "you won't believe what happened" calls.

I don't want us to become those people who only say, "Remember when?"

I want us to still be the people who say,

"Guess what happened today?" 🥹❤️

Because some things don't have to end just because life changes.

And you should know this:

I will still choose you.

Just like you became my person without either of us planning it, I hope you always know that you will have me.

Through the exciting days. Through the messy ones. Through new beginnings. Through bad decisions that I will probably have to hear about. 😂 Through everything.

You don't have to be in Room 103
for me to be your roommate anymore. ♡

You will always be my first roommate.

My favourite gossip partner.

The person I want to tell everything to.

My bestie.

My sister.

My person.

I hope this new chapter brings you everything you deserve. I hope you meet amazing people, make beautiful memories, grow into everything you dream of becoming, and always remember how loved you are.

And when things get overwhelming, remember that somewhere there is still one girl from Room 103 who will happily listen to every tiny detail of your day.

So please... don't forget your Room 103 person.

To that room.
To those late nights.
To those conversations.
To those laughs.
To all the chaos.
And most importantly... to you. ❤️

Thank you for being there.

Thank you for listening to me. For putting up with me. For giving me advice. For gossiping with me. For laughing with me. For roasting me. For being my safe place.

Thank you for turning a room into a home.

And thank you for making these four years something I will carry with me for the rest of my life.

Forever my first roommate.
Forever my favourite person.
Forever my bestie and sister. ❤️

Love you so, so much, thangam. 🫂❤️

And no matter how much things change...

some things will always stay the same.

I guess some things will never change.

Forever your Room 103 person, Ravee ♡
